# Notes about the company and the role

The company builds a mobile banking app for small businesses in Poland and Ukraine. About 300 thousand monthly active users, the iOS app is written in Swift and SwiftUI, the backend is Kotlin with PostgreSQL. The team ships to the App Store every two weeks.

The role is a Senior iOS Engineer in the payments squad: four iOS engineers, two backend engineers, a designer and a product manager. Main goals for the next year are instant payments, a new onboarding flow and improving crash-free sessions above 99.8 percent.

Interview process: a recruiter call, a technical interview about Swift and architecture, a system design round about a payments feature, and a final conversation with the head of mobile about culture and expectations.

Questions to ask the interviewer: how the team measures quality, how much time goes to technical debt, how on-call works for mobile engineers, and what the biggest challenge of the payments squad is right now.

My motivation for this company: a product that real businesses use every day, a strong mobile team that owns its features end to end, and the chance to work on payments at a larger scale than in my current job.
